App Title: Mix Max

App Short Description:
Stop guessing, start optimizing. Mix Max uses an intelligent algorithm to mathematically find the best combination of variables — whether you're perfecting a cocktail recipe or fine-tuning a complex process.

App Main Features:
- Mix six parameter types in one experiment: numbers, durations, temperatures, on/off toggles, multiple-choice options, and even the order things are done in
- Smart units that do the math for you — temperatures convert live between °C, °F and K; durations read as 1h 30m and come with a built-in countdown timer that keeps running even if you close the app, so you can time a step to the suggested length
- Optimize for several goals at once — set each outcome to maximize or minimize and weight how much it matters, so Mix Max balances competing priorities (e.g. tastier *and* cheaper)
- Let an intelligent algorithm suggest the next run to try, converging on the best mix in far fewer attempts than random trial and error
- Lock the values you're happy with and let Mix Max tune only the rest, or tweak any suggested run before you commit to it
- Grade each result against your own scoring guide so ratings stay consistent across every run
- Automatically surfaces the Best Mix discovered so far, with full run history you can revisit, share, or remove

App Long Description:
Ever wonder what the perfect ratio of ingredients makes the best cocktail? Or which combination of settings produces the best outcome in whatever you're working on? Mix Max takes the guesswork out of it — giving anyone a mathematical, systematic way to find the best result from a set of variables.

**The Flow**

1. **Create an Experiment**
   Start by defining an experiment — give it a name and the outcomes you care about. You can optimize for more than one at a time (say, flavor *and* cost), set each to maximize or minimize, and weight how much each one matters so Mix Max knows your priorities.

2. **Define Parameters**
   List the variables that influence your experiment. Mix Max handles six kinds: plain numbers, durations (formatted as 1h 30m, with a built-in countdown timer to run a step for exactly that long), temperatures (that convert between °C, °F and K for you), on/off toggles with your own labels, multiple-choice options, and even the order steps are performed in. Set ranges, increments, and units — these are the knobs Mix Max learns to tune.

3. **Run with an Intelligent Algorithm**
   Instead of testing combinations at random, Mix Max intelligently chooses what to try next, converging toward the optimum in far fewer trials than brute-force or grid search. Already know one value works? Freeze it and let Mix Max tune only the rest — or edit any suggested run before you commit.

4. **Log Your Results**
   After each run, score the outcomes — against your own grading guide so ratings stay consistent — and Mix Max builds a probabilistic model of how your parameters drive the result, getting smarter with every data point. Every run keeps a snapshot of the exact setup it was measured with, so your history stays accurate even as the experiment evolves.

5. **Find the Best Combination**
   Mix Max continuously surfaces the Best Mix discovered so far across all your priorities. Revisit the full run history any time, and share a winning recipe or an entire experiment with anyone.

**Who It's For**
Mix Max is for everyone. You don't need a science degree to use it. If you've ever wanted to know "what's the best possible version of this?" — whether that's a drink recipe, a workout routine, a cooking technique, a product formula, or an engineering process — Mix Max gives you a structured, mathematical way to find the answer. Experiment smarter, not harder.
